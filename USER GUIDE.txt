RaceVision v1.2.0 - TV director and broadcast overlays for NASCAR Racing 2003 Season
==============================================================================

RaceVision directs the TV view while you watch a race. It reads the race straight from
NR2003's memory and cuts to the best thing happening: crashes, passes and close battles
for position, pit stops, the leader, restarts. It picks the camera for each shot, holds
shots long enough to watch them, and draws broadcast-style graphics over the game: a
driver tag, a battle box and an in-car dash on on-board cameras.

Please send racevision.log (next to the exe) with any problem report: it records every
cut RaceVision made and why.


WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2.0 (since 1.1.0)
--------------------------------
- Lead battle hold: the director holds its view when cars are battling for the lead,
  so a door-to-door fight for first stays on screen instead of cutting away.

- Configurable accent color: the plate fade color behind driver names (previously
  fixed teal) is now configurable as dash_accent2. Set it to your team's color or
  leave it at the default.

- Per-driver dash colors: RaceVision picks each car's dash color from the art folder's
  colors.json, so each driver's in-car graphics use their own scheme.

- In-car lens and angles apply live: adjustments to camera lens, angle, and position
  now take effect immediately without reloading.

- Drone near clip fixed: the drone camera no longer clips through the track surface
  when looking straight down at close range.

- Director view (browser): open :8090/director in a browser while RaceVision is
  running to see a timeline recorder for the director's cuts, with a track map,
  car positions, and the cut log updating live. Recordings save to
  %PROGRAMDATA%\NR2003 Companions\racevision\timelines.

- Series art: team logo, driver headshot and number cards now appear on the track map,
  car list and director timeline in the desktop window.

- Camera sets: swap whole sets of the game's own track and car cameras from the camsets folder (a menu, or the
  Cameras drawer in the director view). The first time a file is changed its original is saved, and the
  "stock" set puts it back. Presets for 14 tracks and a series set are included.
- Dynamic cameras: RaceVision's own static cameras on the pit lane camera blocks, learned per track.
- The companion app folder is a setting (companion_folder in racevision.ini). There is no built-in
  default: set it if you use that app's series art or active series.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.1.0 (since 1.0)
---------------------------------
- Crash replays (auto and manual, up to 3 angles, Replays page, Highlight reel,
  Ctrl+F7 in-car trigger)
- Cameras: In-car right, Drone, Side tracking, Low rear, Front quarter, Overhead,
  Pack view, Rear (rides the car ahead). Phone Adjust edits any camera live.
- Director leaves your view alone while you drive; qualifying puts you in the cockpit.
- Two presets: "RaceVision default" and "JT's Customs" (this racevision.ini).


GETTING STARTED
1. Unzip the RaceVision folder anywhere and run RaceVision.exe as administrator.
2. Start a race in NR2003 (before or after RaceVision, either works).
3. For the graphics, run NR2003 windowed or borderless.

RaceVision needs nothing but NR2003 1.2.0.1. It works alongside AIMOD and RaceControl.


THE WINDOW
- Live: what's on screen and why, the camera, the race and track, the running order
  (double-click a car to put it on TV yourself) and the director's log.
- Director, Battles, Cameras: shot hold times, battle thresholds, camera assignments.
- Overlays: which graphics show and when. The in-car dash shows on every on-board shot.
- Art: number cards, headshots and driver colours.
- Presets: save and switch all settings. Presets are .ini files in the presets folder.
- Phone: pair a phone to control RaceVision from it.

Keys (work anywhere, also while NR2003 has focus):
  Ctrl+F9   graphics on / off
  Ctrl+F10  move the graphics (drag, mouse wheel to resize, Ctrl+F10 again to finish)
  Ctrl+F11  pause / resume directing
  Ctrl+F12  quit RaceVision

KNOWN LIMITS
- Pit Exit High / Low cameras (30, 32 in JT's Customs) were built at Talladega_2023
  and sit at fixed world positions - pick other cameras for pit exit at other tracks.
- Director view requires a browser open at :8090/director while RaceVision is running.
  It does not need an internet connection.
